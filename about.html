Who we are

A reliable support partner for people, operations and growth.

HOT Business Solutions Inc. supports exceptional teams across Australia and New Zealand. We help partners scale operations, strengthen service delivery and build sustainable Manila capability.

Unlike a traditional outsourcing model, HBS operates as an extension of the partner’s onshore team. The business unit directs daily priorities while HBS supports employment, HR, payroll, IT, facilities, engagement and continuity.
