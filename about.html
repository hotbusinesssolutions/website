Who we are

A reliable support partner for people, operations and growth.

HOT Business Solutions Inc. is an emerging and growing company in the Philippines and a proud part of the House of Travel Group. From Manila, we support exceptional teams across Australia and New Zealand while helping partners strengthen service delivery and build sustainable capability.

Unlike a traditional outsourcing model, HBS operates as an extension of the partner’s onshore team. The business unit directs daily priorities while HBS supports employment, HR, payroll, IT, facilities, engagement and continuity.

Our story

Experience carried forward.

Our Manila presence began in 2012. After years of working through an outsourced model, the Group took the next step by establishing its own Philippine company—creating stronger connections, more direct support and greater opportunities for growth.

Experienced team members from the earlier operation joined HBS on this journey, carrying forward the expertise, relationships and knowledge built over more than a decade. That continuity gives HBS the energy of a growing company, backed by the stability and experience of an established business.
