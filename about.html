WHO WE ARE

Local expertise. Group-backed stability.

HOT Business Solutions Inc. is an emerging Philippine company and a proud part of the House of Travel Group, supporting exceptional teams across Australia and New Zealand.

Our Manila presence began in 2012. After years of outsourced operations, we established our own local company to strengthen relationships, provide better support and create greater opportunities for growth. Experienced team members carried their knowledge and connections forward into HBS.
