Frequently asked questions

Useful answers, up front.

Does HBS manage daily work?

The partner business unit directs daily activities, objectives and outcomes; HBS supports the employment and operating environment.

Where are teams based?

Teams operate from HBS’s managed workspace in BGC, Metro Manila, subject to the agreed delivery model.

Can a team scale over time?

Yes. The model is designed for flexibility, with growth and role changes agreed through governance and workforce planning.

Which processes are suitable for a Manila team?

Repeatable or clearly calibratable work is usually the best fit, including travel operations, customer support, finance administration, shared services and workplace-enabled support.

How is service quality governed?

Each engagement can define service measures, quality checks, reporting rhythms, escalation paths and regular reviews so performance stays visible to both teams.

How are data security and access managed?

Access is shaped around client-approved tools, role requirements and least-privilege principles, with device, workspace and security needs confirmed during discovery.

Can HBS support a phased team launch?

Yes. A team can begin with a defined pilot or first cohort, then expand in planned phases as roles, training, systems and operating readiness are confirmed.

What should we prepare before discovery?

Bring the role scope, expected volumes, coverage hours, required systems, data or security constraints, performance measures and target start date. HBS can help refine any gaps.
