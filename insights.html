Frequently asked questions

Useful answers, up front.

Does HBS manage daily work?

The partner business unit directs daily activities, objectives and outcomes; HBS supports the employment and operating environment.

Where are teams based?

Teams operate from HBS’s managed workspace in BGC, Metro Manila, subject to the agreed delivery model.

Can a team scale over time?

Yes. The model is designed for flexibility, with growth and role changes agreed through governance and workforce planning.
