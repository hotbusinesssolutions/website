Recruitment FAQs

Before you apply.

What happens after I apply?

Shortlisted applicants are contacted for an initial conversation and, when relevant, a role-specific assessment.

Where will I work?

Roles are based at the managed HBS workplace in BGC unless the vacancy states otherwise.

How do I apply?

Open the available positions page, choose a role that matches your experience and submit the requested application details through JobStreet.

What should I prepare?

Prepare an updated résumé and examples of relevant experience. Some roles may also request a short skills assessment or supporting work samples.

Which shifts or time zones apply?

Schedules depend on the partner and role. The vacancy and recruitment conversation will explain the expected working hours before you proceed.

Do I need travel-industry experience?

Travel knowledge is important for some specialist roles, while other opportunities value transferable customer, finance, people, technology or administrative experience.

How long does recruitment take?

Timing varies by role, assessment needs and start date. The recruitment team will explain the next step and keep shortlisted candidates updated.

How will I know my application status?

Shortlisted candidates will be contacted using the details supplied with their application. Keep those details current and monitor your email and phone.
