Expanded business support scope

Support that can grow with the brief.

HBS is structured to support House of Travel companies and independent third-party clients across Australia, New Zealand and other international markets. Each engagement is validated against the required people, systems, controls and delivery model.

01

Customer & Contact Centre

Voice, email, chat, customer correspondence, sales assistance and service workflows designed around approved channels and service levels.

02

Data & Back-Office Operations

Data management, administrative processing, documentation, reconciliation and repeatable operational tasks supported by defined controls.

03

IT & Managed Technology

Information-technology support, managed workplace services, endpoint and infrastructure coordination, system administration and service continuity.

04

Automation & AI-Enabled Support

Carefully scoped automation and AI-assisted workflows that improve consistency or reduce manual effort, subject to client approval, security and governance.

05

Finance, Accounting & HR

Finance operations, accounting support, payroll inputs, HR administration, employee-lifecycle coordination and shared-service processes.

06

Platforms, Tools & Digital Services

Development, operation and maintenance of approved systems, platforms and tools necessary to deliver the agreed business-support service.

Important: These categories describe the company’s permitted support scope. Final availability, technology, staffing, compliance and implementation requirements are confirmed during discovery.
