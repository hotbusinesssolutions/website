Onboarding process

Four stages to go-live.

01

Calibration

Role clarity, requirements, skills, coverage and performance criteria.

  • Confirm the role profile and responsibilities
  • Agree coverage, systems and working hours
  • Set launch measures and governance
Outcome: approved launch brief
02

Recruitment

Sourcing, screening, background checks and selection.

  • Search against the agreed capability profile
  • Coordinate interviews and assessments
  • Complete checks, offer and start planning
Outcome: selected team and start plan
03

Training

HBS onboarding plus partner-led process and systems readiness.

  • Prepare workplace, equipment and access
  • Complete HBS and partner onboarding
  • Run process training and readiness checks
Outcome: team ready for supported delivery
04

Stabilisation

Ramp-up, check-ins and early performance reviews.

  • Monitor early service and employee experience
  • Resolve launch issues through shared check-ins
  • Move into the agreed business-as-usual rhythm
Outcome: stable operating rhythm
